Social sign-in

Ticket list and local data

  1. The ticket screen checks the server and downloads or removes tickets
  2. After a phone change, tickets are downloaded again from history
  3. On iOS, FMDB / SQLCipher encrypts local data with the key in the Keychain
  4. On Android, FCM updates entry status in real time
